Questions we are asked first

Does any of our data leave our site?

No. The software runs on your own hardware, inside your own boundary. Recordings, measurements and reports stay there. There is no telemetry and no cloud dependency. Licensing is a signed key read on the box, not a call home.

Does it use a language model, and where does it run?

Yes. An assistant drives the exhaustive part of the analysis, but every number comes from signal-processing code, never from the model's say-so. The software is designed to run where your network allows: with a hosted model, with a model you already operate, or entirely on the box.

Does it decide what a signal is or how it should be classified?

No. It measures, groups and describes, and it states the evidence. An engineer accepts, corrects or marks each result unsure. Classification and policy decisions stay with people.

Does it decode or read the content of transmissions?

No. The product works from a signal's physical footprint — timing, bandwidth, structure, modulation family and the links between emissions. Content is never decoded.

What hardware does it need?

A workstation-class analysis box. We supply one at cost, specify one you can buy, or install on hardware you already have. The product is analysis software for recordings; it is not a receiver.

How do we try it?

Send one recording — unclassified, one you own or are authorized to hold — and a description comes back. Or ask us what is in the field today for the module you care about and we will tell you plainly.
