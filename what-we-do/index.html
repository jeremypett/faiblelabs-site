What we do

Analysis software for RF and signal-processing engineers that is useful on day one and never phones home.

Signal analysis on your premises

Our tools read a recording, find and group the signals in it, measure them, and give the engineer a short, honest list of what is there — including a plain “can’t tell” where the data does not support an answer. Everything runs on hardware you control.

Built to be trusted

  • Nothing leaves. Indexing, inference and reporting happen on-site. There is no telemetry and no cloud dependency.
  • The tool flags; people decide. We support an engineer’s judgement; we do not replace it.
  • Fail closed. When the software is unsure whether something is sensitive, it treats it as sensitive.

How we work with customers

We deliver as licensed software on your own machines or on an analysis appliance we configure, with modules enabled by licence. We also take on scoped analysis engagements. If that sounds like your problem, get in touch.
